# Image credits

Every photo on the site, with its source. All are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, with no attribution required. We credit the photographers anyway.

## How these were prepared

- Each photo was chosen to match its slot in `docs/image-list.md`. Alt text in `src/data/images.ts` describes what is actually in the photo.
- Downloaded already cropped to the slot’s aspect ratio and size (2400 px wide for the hero, 1600 or 1200 px for the rest), so no pixels are wasted.
- Re-encoded with sharp (MozJPEG, quality 82, progressive, metadata stripped). Total source size is 6.5 MB, down from 11 MB as downloaded.
- At build time, `astro:assets` generates AVIF and WebP at 480–2400 px widths. Browsers download only the size they need.

## Checks

- Free Unsplash photos only (no Unsplash+ or premium images).
- No identifiable faces; where people appear, only hands are shown.
- Photos posted by kitchen or bathroom renovation companies were avoided, so no real firm’s work is presented as this business’s projects. A few come from property photography studios (Point3D Commercial Imaging, 43 Clicks North), which is a photographer credit, not a fitter’s portfolio.
- Before/after pairs are photos of different rooms, chosen to tell the story. For a real client, replace them with genuine before/after shots from the same position.
