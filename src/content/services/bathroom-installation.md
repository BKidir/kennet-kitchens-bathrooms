A bathroom is a small room with a lot going on. Water, drainage, electrics, ventilation and tiling all have to come together within a few square metres, in a few days, while the family carries on living around the work. It is the coordination that makes or breaks a bathroom, and it is why we never hand any part of it to a subcontractor.

## What’s included in a full bathroom installation

- **Design visit.** We measure, check the soil stack, water pressure and boiler type, and talk about how the room is used. You get a layout plan and 3D visual.
- **Strip-out.** Old suite, tiles and flooring removed and taken away. Pipework capped safely.
- **Plumbing.** New supply and waste pipework, valves, a thermostatic shower and connections to your boiler or cylinder.
- **Electrics.** Extractor fan, lighting, shaver points, illuminated mirrors and underfloor heating, installed and certified by our NICEIC-registered electrician.
- **Boarding and waterproofing.** Cement or foam boards in wet areas, with tanking behind showers and baths.
- **Tiling.** Walls and floors laid by our own tiler. See our [tiling service](/tiling) for the details that make the difference.
- **Fitting.** Bath, shower, toilet, basin, furniture, towel rail, screens and accessories.
- **Finishing.** Silicone, grout sealing, painting if included, a full clean and a walk-round with you.

## Getting the layout right

Most Swindon family bathrooms are between 4 and 6 square metres. In that space, small decisions make a big difference:

- A **shower bath** or a bath with a fixed screen keeps a bath for the children and gives the adults a proper shower.
- A **wall-hung vanity** shows more floor, which makes the room feel bigger and is easier to clean.
- **Concealed cisterns** behind a tiled stud wall give you a useful shelf and a cleaner look.
- **Moving the door** or swapping it for an outward-opening one can free up a whole wall.
- **Recessed niches** in the shower area replace those wobbly corner caddies.

We show you the options in 3D, so you can see how much space each one gives you before you decide.

## How long it takes

A typical eight to twelve-day bathroom installation follows a set order: strip-out, first-fix plumbing and electrics, boarding and tanking, tiling, then fitting and finishing. Our [bathroom installation timeline](/advice/how-long-does-a-bathroom-installation-take) walks through it week by week.

If it is your only bathroom, we plan around that: the toilet is reconnected as quickly as possible and we tell you in advance which day the water will be off.
