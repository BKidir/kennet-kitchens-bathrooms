If you have ever had a queue on the landing on a school morning, you already know why a downstairs toilet matters. It is also one of the few home improvements that estate agents consistently say buyers look for, especially in three and four-bedroom family homes.

## Where we put new cloakrooms

- **Under the stairs.** The classic option in Swindon’s semis and terraces. The sloping ceiling means planning carefully so you can sit and stand comfortably.
- **In a utility room.** Partitioning off part of a utility gives a toilet and a laundry area that share plumbing.
- **From a hallway cupboard or old coal store.** Common in 1930s homes around Old Town and Rodbourne.
- **In a garage conversion.** Often as part of a larger project with a boot room.

## Making a small room work

Cloakrooms are tiny, so every centimetre counts. Our usual toolkit:

- **Short-projection and back-to-wall toilets** that save valuable space in front of the pan.
- **Corner or slimline basins**, sometimes as narrow as 25cm, with a wall-mounted tap to save even more depth.
- **Concealed cisterns** behind a boxed-in shelf for a neater finish.
- **An outward-opening or pocket door** so nobody has to squeeze past the door to sit down.
- **A good fan.** Cloakrooms rarely have a window, so we always fit quiet, effective extraction ducted to the outside.

## Have fun with it

A cloakroom is the perfect place to be bold. Because it is small, a striking wallpaper, dark green tiles, a patterned floor or a statement brass tap doesn’t cost the earth and doesn’t overwhelm. Many of our customers choose a more adventurous look here than anywhere else in the house, and it is often the room guests remember.

## What the work involves

1. **Survey.** We check headroom, the route to the soil stack, the water supply and where the fan will vent.
2. **Building and drainage.** Stud walls if needed, new waste pipe, water supply and electrics for the fan and lights.
3. **Waterproofing and boarding.** Moisture-resistant boards behind the basin and toilet.
4. **Tiling and flooring.** Laid by our own [tiling](/tiling) team, who take extra care with cuts in small spaces where every one is on show.
5. **Fitting and finishing.** Toilet, basin, tap, mirror, lighting and accessories, then a full clean.
