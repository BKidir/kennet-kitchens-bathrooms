Chippenham is a busy market town on the River Avon, with a historic centre of Bath stone buildings and plenty of newer family homes on the edges of town. It sits at the western end of the area we cover, and we’re here most weeks fitting kitchens and bathrooms.

## The homes we see in Chippenham

**Bath stone terraces and townhouses** near the centre and around the Market Place have plenty of character but also solid walls, small back kitchens and the occasional damp issue. We survey these carefully and design kitchens that make the most of narrow rear rooms.

**Post-war and 1970s homes** in areas such as Pewsham and Monkton Park often still have their original layouts. Knocking a small kitchen into a dining room is one of the most popular projects here, creating space for a family table or an island.

**1990s and 2000s estates** like Cepen Park and Hunters Moon have kitchens that are now 20 to 30 years old. Some need a complete [kitchen installation](/kitchen-installation); others only need new doors and worktops through our [refurbishment service](/kitchen-refurbishment).

**New-build homes** on the expanding edges of town tend to have small family bathrooms and en-suites that customers want to upgrade to walk-in showers and better storage.

## A Chippenham kitchen

In a 2000s detached home in Cepen Park, the original kitchen had worn doors, swollen carcasses under the sink and a cramped corner that wasted space. We redesigned it as an L-shape with a bank of integrated appliances, replaced the corner cupboard with a pull-out system and fitted pale stone-effect [worktops](/kitchen-worktops) against light grey doors. The project took just under three weeks on site.

## Bathroom renovations in Chippenham

Family bathrooms are our most common Chippenham project. A typical job swaps a shower-over-bath for a separate walk-in shower or a bath with a proper fixed screen, adds a wall-hung vanity for storage, and finishes the room with large-format [tiles](/tiling). For homeowners planning ahead, we also build level-access [wet rooms](/wet-rooms).
