Royal Wootton Bassett has a lovely mix of homes, from the Georgian and Victorian buildings along the High Street to the family estates that grew around the town from the 1970s onwards. It also has a good number of bungalows, which is one reason we fit so many accessible bathrooms and wet rooms here.

## Homes we work on in Royal Wootton Bassett

**Period properties near the High Street** often have narrow rooms, solid walls and steps between floor levels. Kitchens here need careful planning to make the most of the space, and we frequently design around a chimney breast or an awkward back door.

**1970s to 1990s family homes** in areas such as Noremarsh, Lime Kiln and Brynards Hill make up much of our work in the town. Common projects include knocking through to a dining room for a bigger family kitchen, adding a utility, and replacing a tired family bathroom with a separate shower and bath.

**Newer homes** around Woodshaw and the edges of town tend to have good-sized kitchens that simply need updating. A [kitchen refurbishment](/kitchen-refurbishment) with new doors and quartz worktops is often all they need.

**Bungalows** around the town and in nearby villages like Broad Town and Brinkworth are ideal for [accessible and walk-in bathrooms](/accessible-bathrooms). Many of our customers here are planning ahead, making the bathroom safer before it becomes urgent.

## A local project: making a bungalow bathroom safe

One of our favourite recent projects was in a 1980s bungalow in the town. The homeowner could no longer step into her bath safely after a fall. We broke out the concrete floor, re-laid the waste and built a level-access [wet room](/wet-rooms) with a fold-down seat, discreet grab rails, a widened doorway and underfloor heating. Her family say it looks more like a hotel than an adapted bathroom.
